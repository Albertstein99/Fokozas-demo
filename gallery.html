A world of geometry.

Drag the purple triangle vertices or the purple point on a circle. Gray points and auxiliary curves follow the construction. Pan empty space, scroll to zoom, or use the fit button. Triangle sides and auxiliary lines are shown as complete lines. Collinear triangles have no finite circumcenter or orthocenter.
